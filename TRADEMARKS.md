# Name, Branding & Artwork Policy

The **code** in the Equestria OS repositories is free software under the
[GNU GPL v3 or later](LICENSE). You may study it, change it, and share it —
including in a fork — as long as you follow the GPL.

The GPL covers code. It does **not** cover the project's identity. This file
explains what that means in practice.

## The Equestria OS name and identity

"Equestria OS", "Psyche Games", the Equestria OS logo, and the project's
official channels (the `equestria-os-packages` pacman repository, the
`equestria-os-keyring` signing keys, release ISOs) identify the official
project. They are not licensed to you by the GPL.

**You may, without asking:**

* Redistribute unmodified official packages and ISOs, and run mirrors of them.
* Write about, review, stream, or teach Equestria OS under its name.
* Say that your project is "based on Equestria OS" or "a fork of Equestria OS".

**If you distribute a modified version** (a fork, a respin, a derived
distribution), you must:

* Give it a different name, and not present it as Equestria OS or as official.
* Remove or replace the Equestria OS logo and the `equestria-os-branding`
  package.
* Use your own package repository and your own signing keys.

If you are unsure, or want an exception — open an Issue and ask. Improvements
sent back to the main project are always preferred over a separate fork; see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Artwork

Wallpapers, character art, and other artwork shipped with Equestria OS belong
to their respective artists. They are **not** covered by the GPL and are
**not** the property of this project. Do not reuse them outside Equestria OS
without the permission of their authors.
